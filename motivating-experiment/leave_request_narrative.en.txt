So in general it starts when an employee sends me a message to tell me they would like to take leave. Sometimes it's just two days, sometimes it's three weeks in a row, it really depends. Well, before doing anything I look at their balance, because it has already happened to me that I approved something and realised afterwards that the person didn't have enough days left, so now I always check first.
If the balance is not enough, I tell them directly that it won't work like that, and then either they withdraw their request, or we discuss an arrangement, like unpaid leave, but that has to be approved by management, it's not me who decides that.
If the balance is fine, normally I forward the request to the person's direct manager, he is the one who has to give the green light because he knows the team's workload. Sometimes he approves right away, sometimes he tells me to wait because there is a project going on and he can't afford to have someone absent at that moment. In that case the request stays pending and I talk about it again with the employee to see if they can move their dates.
Once the manager has said yes, there is a special case: if the request exceeds ten consecutive days, you also need the approval of the n+2, so the manager's manager in a way, otherwise it doesn't go through, whatever the direct manager says. It's an internal rule, I don't really know why but that's how it is.
Once I have all the necessary green lights, I record the leave in the payroll system so that it is taken into account on the payslip, and I send a confirmation to the employee with the approved dates. And that's it, it's done, unless they change their mind in the meantime, but that's another story.
